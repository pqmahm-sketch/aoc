L. Opening & Awarding Flow Parade

Participant Parade Flow:

  1. Participant stand by on back stage (divided by 2, every country)
  2. All participants from same country move to center stage
  3. When MC announced the Nation, participant move forward to the front of stage
  4. Participant continue to their seat

Catatan Penting:

  • Participant in the Center Stage will bring the Nation Flag (90x60 cm), the others bring small Nation Flag

Urutan Negara:

Cambodia → India → Indonesia → Malaysia → Maldives → Philippines → Singapore → Taiwan → Thailand → Vietnam
